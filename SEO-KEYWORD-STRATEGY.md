## 1. Search Intent Matrix Overview

To capture high-intent buyers, founders, corporate directors, and event attendees, the search keyword strategy is categorized across four distinct intent buckets:
1. **Commercial Intent (High Conversion)**: Users seeking to purchase an NFC business card or subscribe to a digital identity platform.
2. **Informational Intent (Top-of-Funnel Education)**: Users researching NFC technology, compatibility with iPhones, vCard functionality, and green alternatives to paper cards.
3. **Geo-Targeted Intent (India Market Penetration)**: Queries focused on local currency (INR), shipping, metro business hubs (Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad), and UPI payment support.
4. **Long-Tail Intent (High-Relevance Niche)**: Specific use cases such as metal executive cards, real estate networking cards, doctor digital cards, and multi-brand identity setups.

## 3. On-Page Keyword Placement Strategy

To maximize relevance and avoid search engine keyword stuffing penalties:
1. **Title Tags (`<title>`)**: Primary head keyword at the start, followed by secondary modifier and `| ${brandConfig.name}`.
   * Example: `NFC Business Card — Smart Contactless Tap Cards | TAPTCH`
2. **Meta Descriptions**: Active voice, includes primary keyword, unique value proposition (vCard download, zero app required), and a clear call to action.
3. **Heading Hierarchy**:
   * `<h1>`: Single instance per page containing the core search topic and user benefit.
   * `<h2>`: Specific feature, material, or benefit clusters containing secondary keywords.
   * `<h3>`: Individual component or specification items (e.g., "Genuine NXP NTAG216 Chips").
4. **Semantic Content Optimization**: Natural integration of related entities (RFID, NDEF, vCard, QR code, iOS Background Tag Reading, ferrite layer, DPDP Act).
